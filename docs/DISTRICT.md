# Flatiron district

The city in FLY is a small piece of the Flatiron neighborhood in Manhattan.

The footprints and street centerlines come from OpenStreetMap, fetched from
the map API for the box 40.7400°N–40.7432°N, 73.9918°W–73.9878°W. Most
building heights are the OSM `height` tag. Where that tag is missing, the
model uses `building:levels` times 3.5 m, or 18 m. Coordinates are metres
from 40.74155°N, 73.98970°W, with +x east and +y north.

© OpenStreetMap contributors. The data is available under the
[Open Database License](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).

This is not a survey and it is not for navigation. Facades, window grids,
setbacks, and roof equipment are drawn procedures on top of those
footprints. Collision uses the footprint extruded to the roof, not the
drawn setback. The launch point is an open patch near Madison Square. The
landing pad is one real mid-rise roof in the extract, marked in amber.
